About

Meet Mahalakshmi Subbaraman

Mahalakshmi is an international management trainer, consultant, and counsellor with over 22 years of experience. She has conducted more than 2,000 corporate training and growth assignments across India and abroad — exceptionally highly rated for quality.

Trained under spiritual master and motivational speaker Swami Sukhabodhananda, and grounded in modern management techniques, she brings a unique perspective: infusing spiritual learning into the corporate world to help maximise the potential of executives and employees.

She has a track record of achievement from an early stage — excelling in sports and arts, and topping at the state level in her diploma engineering studies. She is a reputed entrepreneur in the corporate training field, and holds a position as visiting faculty at KPR Institute of Engineering & Technology.

Programs

Signature Program

Personal Effectiveness Program (PEP)

The cornerstone offering — a transformational program that has been delivered to thousands of professionals across manufacturing, banking, healthcare, public sector, and education. PEP works at the level of identity, not just skills: participants leave with a clearer sense of purpose, sharper decision-making, and a framework for sustained personal effectiveness.

  • Clarity of purpose and values
  • Stronger ownership & accountability
  • Better decision-making under pressure
  • Resilience and emotional steadiness
  • Sustained behavioural change post-program
