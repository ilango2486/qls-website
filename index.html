About

Meet Mahalakshmi Subbaraman

Mahalakshmi is an international management trainer, consultant, and counsellor with over 22 years of experience. She has conducted more than 1,000 corporate training and growth assignments across India and abroad — exceptionally highly rated for quality.

Trained under spiritual master and motivational speaker Swami Sukhabodhananda, and grounded in modern management techniques, she brings a unique perspective: infusing spiritual learning into the corporate world to help maximise the potential of executives and employees.

She has a track record of achievement from an early stage — excelling in sports and arts, and topping at the state level in her diploma engineering studies. She is a reputed entrepreneur in the corporate training field, with engagements ranging from in-house workshops to large-scale public webinars.

Signature Program

Personal Effectiveness Program (PEP)

PEP is designed exclusively for the corporate sector — transforming employees at a deep core level. It works on the understanding that people's behaviour is an expression of their attitudes, which in turn are a product of their core personality. The program produces long-lasting attitudinal and behavioural improvements.

Effectiveness

Learning the right skills to do the right things — outer management of the external world.

Efficiency

Core personality transformation to do them the right way — inner management of the internal self.

What participants work on

  • Mind Management
  • Managing Mindsets
  • Handling Conflicts
  • Handling Emotions — Anger, Ego, Jealousy
  • Being Proactive
  • Looking Good, Feeling Good, Being Good
  • Being in the Present
